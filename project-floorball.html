/ The project

Built for the game.

Tournament management system that I created to facilitate management of an annual floorball tournament.

Instead of managing results, standings and progression manually, the website handles the tournament logic and keeps everything visible in one interface.

/ Key features
01

Group play

Matches are organised into a clear group stage with the tournament schedule visible in one place.

02

Live standings

Team positions are calculated from match results and updated automatically.

03

Goal difference

Goals scored and conceded are used to calculate the current goal difference.

04

Playoffs

The tournament progresses from the group stage into semifinals and a final.

/ How it works

From groups to final.

01
Group stage

Teams play their scheduled group matches.

02
Results

Match results feed directly into the tournament standings.

03
Standings

Points and goal difference determine the current ranking.

04
Playoffs

Qualified teams move through the semifinal and final stages.
